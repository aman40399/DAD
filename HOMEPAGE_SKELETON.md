## Position Details

| Position | Section | Style | Importance |
|----------|---------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Breaking News Banner | Horizontal banner | Urgent news |
| 2 | Hero Article | Large text, 2/3 width | Most important |
| 3 | Hero Image | Image, 1/3 width | Visual for hero |
| 4-6 | Top Stories | 3-column grid, images | High priority |
| 7-8 | Featured | 2-column, medium cards | Featured content |
| 9-11 | Latest News | 3-column grid | Recent updates |
| 12 | Spotlight | Full-width feature | Special feature |
| 13-16 | Editor's Picks | 4-column, compact | Curated content |
| 17-18 | Trending | 2-column, medium | Popular articles |
| 19-20 | More Stories | 2-column, standard | Additional content |

## Pagination

- **Page 1**: Positions 1-20 (Homepage skeleton)
- **Page 2+**: Standard grid layout, 20 articles per page
- Chronological order for pages 2+
